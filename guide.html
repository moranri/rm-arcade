A little help on your next run.

Choose a game from the collection, read the mission, then select Start game. Every game supports pause (P), restart, and optional sound. Leaving the tab pauses your game automatically.

01 · Afterlight

Move with Left/Right or A/D, and hold Space to shoot. On a touchscreen, drag the ship to move and fire, or use the buttons below the board. Each enemy is worth 50 points. R pickups shorten the firing interval from 0.26 to 0.09 seconds for eight seconds; S gives a five-second shield; F restores 40 fuel. At 1,000 points, the Warden replaces normal enemies. Hit it 32 times and survive its spread and aimed shots to win. Collisions or empty fuel end the run.

02 · Break the Grid

Move with Left/Right or A/D; Space or a tap launches the ball. Paddle contact angle changes the rebound. Clear all three layouts with three lives. Destroyed bricks can drop W (wide paddle), L (slow ball), or T (sticky paddle), each lasting 12 seconds. Sticky catches the ball; use Space to relaunch. A power-up must touch the paddle to activate.

03 · Golden Hour

Move with arrows or WASD, or the four touch buttons. Find eight gold coins in a newly generated maze. The exit is locked until the last coin is collected. Cyan clocks add 30 seconds. Coral guards patrol straight corridors and send you to the entrance if they touch you; your collected coins are kept. Reach the open exit before time runs out.

04 · Signal Keep

Select a Pulse tower (60 energy) or Frost tower (45 energy), then select a numbered pad. Keyboard users can Tab to the pad buttons. Pulse deals damage; Frost slows enemies so Pulse gets more shots. Click an occupied pad to spend 50 energy on an upgrade, up to level three. Start with 180 energy and 15 relay integrity. Press Enter or Send wave when ready. Enemies award energy when defeated; clearing a wave awards 40 more. Survive six waves to win.

Later waves introduce runners and armored intruders. Armored units resist some Pulse damage and cost two integrity if they reach the relay. There is no penalty for taking time between waves to choose upgrades.

Local high scores

When a completed run qualifies for the top five, enter 1–3 letters or numbers and select Save score. Scores are local to this browser and this website address; they do not sync across devices or between Vercel and obi2. If browser storage is blocked, the game keeps a temporary score table for the current tab.

Play with a peer

Take turns using different initials. Ask your tester whether the goal and controls were clear, whether the difficulty felt fair, and whether anything behaved unexpectedly. Record their actual comments in the lab's peer-test sheet.
